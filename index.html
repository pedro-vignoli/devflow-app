DevFlow

DevFlow is a personal automation tool for developers. It publishes the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts on a schedule chosen by the owner.

DevFlow is a single-user tool. It does not offer accounts, does not collect data from visitors and has no advertising.

DevFlow e uma ferramenta pessoal de automacao. Ela publica os videos do proprio dono nas contas do proprio dono, no horario escolhido por ele.

Connect TikTok

Connect TikTok uses TikTok Login Kit (scope user.info.basic) and asks permission to publish videos (scope video.publish) to the owner's own account.
